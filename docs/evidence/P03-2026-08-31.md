## Implementation inventory

- `model.m`: bounded deterministic construction of two paired displacement records. It centers and
  N-normalizes a seeded Sensor A driver, removes Sensor B's mean and projection on A, then mixes the
  shared and orthogonal components transparently. It manually computes means, centered products,
  N-normalized variances/covariance, correlation, the corresponding N−1 covariance, cumulative
  full-record-centered product averages, the raw cross-moment, covariance matrix, empirical
  determinant, analytic positive-semidefinite margin, and same-direction fraction. It validates
  `N` from 3 through 50,000, `rho` from −1 through 1, RMS scales from `1e-6` through `1e6 mm`,
  bounded means and mean-to-scale ratios, and a uint32-compatible seed before allocation. Caller RNG
  state is restored.
- `p03_baseline.m`: one deterministic `N=400`, `rho=0.70`, `sigma_A=1.0 mm`,
  `sigma_B=1.5 mm`, seed-303 transition with paired centered traces, a deviation cloud, cumulative
  centered-product average, labeled units, and summary metrics. Cleanup is limited to P03 figures.
- `experiment.m`: a `rho=[-0.8,0,0.8]` sweep at fixed scales; an independent
  `sigma_B=[0.5,1.5,3.0] mm` sweep at fixed `rho` with common panel limits; mechanism sections after
  each observation; and a broken zero-mean shortcut shown before a separate centering repair. Every
  learner-run section initializes its own inputs.
- `interactive.m`: bounded `rho`, Sensor B RMS scale, and seed controls; two complementary views;
  stable scale-aware scatter limits; a 160-point trace render cap; P03-only UI replacement; and a
  captured P03 model handle so callbacks do not re-resolve a generic `model` after launcher cleanup.
- `run_checks.m`: determinism, independent manual formulas, covariance symmetry, Cauchy-Schwarz,
  analytic/empirical PSD limits, non-vacuous N versus N−1 normalization, `rho=0` and `rho=±1`, two
  lever-isolation checks, translation and scale invariance, raw-cross-moment failure, nonlinear
  zero-covariance interpretation fixture, caller-RNG isolation, malformed inputs, and both resource
  boundaries.
- `tests/test_p03_module.py`: independent Python arithmetic for positive, negative, zero, translated,
  scaled, N versus N−1, constant-record, and nonlinear fixtures. It also retains source-level MATLAB,
  pedagogy, lifecycle, compatibility, and newline contracts. It does not establish MATLAB numerical fidelity.
- `tests/test_learn_cli.py`: manifest-derived learner-flow coverage plus a P03 frontier-rollback
  behavior test that restores P03 to `scaffolded`, resumes at P02, excludes P03 from active counts,
  rejects a new P03 selection, and preserves dormant P03 completion and teach-back data.

## Independent review repairs

- Renamed the cumulative metric so it is not mislabeled as prefix covariance: it is the cumulative
  average of products centered by the complete displayed record.
- Fixed Sensor B scale-sweep and UI axes so autoscaling cannot hide the physical vertical stretch.
- Added an analytic PSD margin and scale-aware determinant tolerances at singular `rho=±1` limits.
- Distinguished this controlled N-normalized record moment from the common N−1 sample estimator and
  added a nonzero independent reference for their ratio.
- Clarified that signed product magnitudes, not a majority of product signs, determine covariance.
- Bounded mean-to-scale ratios to prevent destructive loss of centered variation at extreme offsets.
- Replaced statistical-independence wording with orthogonal/zero-covariance wording and described
  `rho` as a signed shared-component weight rather than a variance fraction.
- Added an explicit nonlinear fixture so zero covariance is not taught as absence of dependence.
- Added the missing P03-specific rollback behavior test; the prior rollback test exercised P02 and did
  not directly support the retained P03 recovery claim.
- Narrowed the top-level pass disposition to local static and independent-reference evidence so it
  cannot be read as P03-specific remote CI or MATLAB-runtime validation.
