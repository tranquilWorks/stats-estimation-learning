# Lesson: Watch Randomness Stabilize with Repetition

## Guiding question

What does repetition reveal about random variation?

## Mental model

Individual random outcomes jump around, but repeated independent measurements reveal stable long-run structure.

## What to manipulate

Use `interactive.m`. Change one lever at a time before combining effects.

## First observation

Increase the number of trials. Watch the running proportion become less volatile while the histogram of repeated counts concentrates around n·p.

## Common mistakes

- Convergence does not mean the next outcome becomes predictable.
- A larger sample reduces estimator variation; it does not change the underlying probability.
- One lucky run is not evidence that the model is correct.

## Completion standard

The learner can explain the baseline, identify what each lever changes, diagnose the deliberately broken case, and pass `run_checks.m`.
